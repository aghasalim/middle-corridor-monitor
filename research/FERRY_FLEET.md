# Caspian rail ferries, Ro-Ro and Ro-Pax on the Middle Corridor lines (13 Nov 2026)

Question: are the ships that carry wagons and trucks across the Caspian counted by PortWatch under "Ro-Ro", or are some registered as passenger ships and so outside its five cargo categories?

**Sources.** Per-vessel data are from VesselFinder detail pages (`https://www.vesselfinder.com/vessels/details/<IMO>`), read on 13 Nov 2026 (**opened**). Each page shows two classifications:
- **Ship Type**: the register-style type (VesselFinder's static database, which looks like IHS/Sea-web naming).
- **AIS Type**: the type code the ship itself broadcasts over AIS.

Length is length overall from the same page. Operator is from the cited news or report, not from a register. The owner/manager fields on VesselFinder were blank.

## What the evidence supports

1. **The Caspian ferry fleet splits into two registry types.**
   - The Soviet-era Uljanik class (DAGISTAN, MERKURY 1, AKADEMIK M. TOPCHUBASHOV, PROFESSOR GUL) and the new CNF18C Ro-Pax ships (AZERBAIJAN, ZARIFA ALIYEVA) are registered as **"Passenger/Ro-Ro Cargo Ship"**. The Turkmen BERKARAR and BAGTYYAR are too.
   - The 2005–2012 series (SHAHDAG, AGDAM, NAKHCHYVAN, BARDA, BALAKEN), which MagicPort labels "Rail Vehicles Carrier", is registered as **"Ro-Ro Cargo Ship"**.
2. **The AIS type codes split the same way.**
   - The Passenger/Ro-Ro ships broadcast "Passenger ship". The Turkmen pair broadcast nonsense codes ("Medical", "HSC").
   - The Ro-Ro Cargo ships broadcast "Cargo ship".
3. **What follows for PortWatch.** PortWatch states five cargo categories, and its method papers exclude passenger vessels (Verschuur et al. 2021 include "only the main trade-carrying vessels"). So the Passenger/Ro-Ro ferries are **likely outside PortWatch's "ro-ro" count**, whether PortWatch classifies by register type or by AIS type. These are about half of the rail-capable fleet, including both new Ro-Pax ships and all Turkmen ferries. If this holds:
   - PortWatch Ro-Ro calls at Baku, Aktau and Turkmenbashi count mainly the five Croatian-built rail ferries plus BESTEKAR GARA GARAYEV.
   - Rough check: 410–518 Ro-Ro calls a year at Baku in 2022–2025 is about 70–90 calls per ship for six ships. That is plausible, but it is my arithmetic, not a verified fact.
   - Turkmenbashi's Ro-Ro calls would come from ASCO's Ro-Ro-registered ferries, not from the Turkmen fleet.
4. **Fleet changes do not explain the 2022 step.** No Ro-Ro-registered ship entered service in 2021–2022. The two newcomers (AZERBAIJAN, ZARIFA ALIYEVA) are Passenger/Ro-Ro.

## What stays unknown

- Which field PortWatch uses to assign the five categories (IHS StatCode, its own mapping, or the AIS type), and whether "Passenger/Ro-Ro Cargo" maps to "ro-ro" or is dropped. No published document says.
- The register type in IHS/S&P Sea-web itself. VesselFinder's naming suggests that source, but I could not open Sea-web or Equasis (both need accounts).
- Whether the ships' AIS type codes changed over 2019–2024, which could also produce a step.
- The full ASCO ferry list: about 5–7 ferries remain unidentified. Also the names and IMO numbers of the MCM Kazakh ferries and the operator of the Turkmen ferries.
